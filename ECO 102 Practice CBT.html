Instructions

  1. Each question has four options (A to D). Only one is correct.
  2. Click an option to select it. Click a different option to change your answer.
  3. Use Next and Previous, or tap a number in the Question Navigator to jump to any question.
  4. Use Flag to mark a question you want to revisit. Flagged questions show in orange.
  5. The countdown clock runs from the moment you start. When it reaches zero your exam is submitted automatically.
  6. There is no negative marking. The pass mark is 50%.
  7. Answers and explanations are shown after you submit, as in a real CBT.
